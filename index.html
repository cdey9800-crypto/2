How to Bake Perfect Sourdough Bread at Home

There's nothing quite like the smell of freshly baked sourdough bread filling your kitchen. With this simple recipe, you'll be baking like a pro in no time.

Ingredients

  • 500g bread flour
  • 350g warm water
  • 100g active sourdough starter
  • 10g fine sea salt

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Mix the flour and water in a large bowl. Let it rest for 30 minutes (this is called autolyse).

Step 2: Add the sourdough starter and salt. Mix until well combined.

Step 3: Knead the dough for 10-15 minutes until smooth and elastic.

Step 4: Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let it rise for 4-6 hours at room temperature.

Step 5: Shape the dough into a round loaf and place it in a proofing basket. Let it proof for 2-3 hours.

Step 6: Preheat your oven to 230°C (450°F) with a Dutch oven inside.

Step 7: Carefully transfer the dough to the hot Dutch oven, score the top, and bake for 20 minutes with the lid on.

Step 8: Remove the lid and bake for another 20-25 minutes until golden brown.

Step 9: Let it cool completely on a wire rack before slicing.

Tips for Success

  • Use a kitchen scale for accurate measurements
  • Feed your starter 4-6 hours before using
  • Don't skip the autolyse step
  • Be patient with the fermentation process
